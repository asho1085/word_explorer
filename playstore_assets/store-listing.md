## Full description (max 4000)

Shabdkhoj is a word search puzzle that helps you learn English words from Hindi. See a Hindi word, find its English meaning on the board, and remember it as you play.

DUO MODE: HINDI → ENGLISH
Each clue is a Hindi word, such as घर, पानी or किताब. Your job is to find its English word (HOME, WATER, BOOK) hidden in the letter grid. Each level teaches everyday words, so you build your English vocabulary as you play.

ENGLISH MODE
A classic English word search. Find animals, objects and everyday words hidden across, down and diagonally.

HINDI MODE: हिन्दी शब्द खोजें
A word search made for Devanagari letters. Find Hindi words like फूल, बादल and हवा in a Hindi letter grid.

FEATURES
• 3 game modes: Duo, English and Hindi
• 150 levels, 50 in each mode
• Hints when you're stuck: in Duo Mode a hint shows the meaning, for example "घर means HOME"
• Earn up to 3 stars per level and unlock the next level
• Collect coins and climb the leaderboard
• Track your stars, levels and daily streak
• Light and dark themes
• Shuffle the board for a new look at the letters
• Play as a guest, no sign-up needed to start

WHO IS IT FOR?
• Hindi speakers who want to learn English words
• Students practising English vocabulary
• Anyone who enjoys word search, word find or crossword-style puzzles
• Players who want a word game in Hindi

HOW TO PLAY
1. Read the clue word at the top of the screen.
2. Swipe across the letters to select the word.
3. Find every word to complete the level and earn stars.

Download Shabdkhoj and learn a new word every time you play.
